## 🌐 ABOUT THE PROJECT

Scam Watch X Nova is a digital threat intelligence and scam awareness platform focused on helping people recognize suspicious activity, understand potential fraud risks, and navigate an increasingly complex digital environment.

The project's vision is to create a unified environment for scam intelligence, threat analysis, suspicious activity awareness, and digital protection.

It aims to make information about digital threats easier to understand, investigate, and share responsibly.

## 🎯 MISSION

To help protect individuals, businesses, and digital communities by improving access to scam intelligence and cybersecurity awareness.
